I chose this aesthetic picture of a calming boat ride in Japan because I really liked how the angle was shot focusing on the red tree with the boat as the background. The lighting of the water reflecting draw our eyes towards the boat is a great focus point. To recreate this environment I choose to go with a pixel-art game type of style and use attributes to merge the squares together without leaving any outlines to create the leaves and I also use the subtraction option to remove areas where the leaves would intersect with the branches. To create the boat I used rhombuses. and parallelograms to create the roof of the boat and the bottom. Instead of making the entire environment full of squares I also used ovals to create the highlights of the water as well as the shadow.
