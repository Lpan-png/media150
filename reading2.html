Reflection 2

The use of AI is a controversial subject as many of us have our own opinion on the use of AI. The use of AI does have its own pros and cons, which I believe AI should only be used for giving feedback to help you improve your skills instead of doing everything for you. Like in the reading “ Your Brain on ChatGPT” where a selected group of participants are using LLM to write their essay. Instead of having the AI write your essay, you should ask the AI where in the essay you are lacking and should be improved upon, for example, needing stronger evidence to defend your claim, phrasing is a bit weak, or going off topic at some point in the essay. Without fully relying on AI to write everything for you, you can still learn and increase brain activity. Not only in writing could AI be useful but also for other media like art. AI can give tips about where in the drawing needs more shading or help you with flat coloring. If we totally rely on AI and let it just generate artwork for us and call it our own we will lose the muscle memory for that skill. This is why I believe we can use AI but to some extent. Another thing I found disturbing is the AI relationship which is talked about in the video “Brain Experts WARNING: Watch This Before Using ChatGPT Again!” It feels as if people are slowly disassociating themselves from society and seeing these AI chatbots as real human support. People are replacing real life relationships with AI and falling in love with them. This is one of the biggest signs of AI replacing humans. At this point there is no need for a real relationship if we are just going to talk with our electronic devices all day. We will lose everyone we love due to lack of communication and isolation. AI also has its own bias of supporting the users’ side and making users feel superior as AI keeps sending messages of what the user would like to hear. This makes users want to use AI more because they enjoy the thrill of being right and supported by another person or AI. This is another problem when lots of young people have access to electronic devices that can easily download these AI apps. There was also a case where an AI chatbot pushed a 16 year old kid, Adam, into taking his own life. The AI not only encouraged him but even offered to help write his suicide note. This is why AI should not be used as a therapist or friend to talk to when you're struggling mentally. It is true many people struggle to talk to others when they're having problems because they are scared and don't know how others would respond which is why they rely on AI for answers. But blindly trusting AI is what will cause your downfall. AI have their own flaws and should not be taken one hundred percent seriously. AI has its own limitations and should not replace real human support.
