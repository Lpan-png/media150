Reflection 3

After reading the motherhood story “ The Devil At My Door” I love how they use the illustrations to show Kadiatu’s past, which later used photographs to show the present day. I believe without the photographs most people wouldn't believe it was a real story unless they see actual proof. The illustrations at the start made me feel a bit unsettled with the use of black background with no humans in the back, it also gives it some kind of an intense moment with text only. As the story progresses we see Kadiatu struggling to keep her kid alive after finally giving birth to him. The use of making the weather a rainy and stormy night makes it even more dramatic and we see a compelling emotion of struggles that she is putting on to save her kid’s life. In some scenes I really like how they used photographs of the actual area as the background to show the less fortunate areas and what their life is like. The scene of Kadiatu crouching down next to the graves of her many dead children was also very touching because you don't know what it's like to experience that many deaths of your children. It makes her feel ashamed for being unable to keep them alive. The story also used some animation, for example when Kadiatu was waking up from her surgery there was an eye opening animation and it made the viewers feel like we are in her perspective and what it was like. These animations also make the story more interactive and make the viewers feel more engaged. Also, during her pregnancy the illustrations did a great job on the aching posture and we the viewers can see how uncomfortable it looks like for her and with the facial expression of her pain. She was shown with multiple frames of feeling discomfort and pain that we feel like we are in that moment and on the journey with her. These long frames did a great story telling and didn't change her expression so subtle that we couldn't tell if her experience was really that painful. Overall, I really like the use of illustration to explain the past that photographs couldn't since it could only be taken in the moment. Illustrations is really good at exaggerating emotions that we don't see a lot of in photographs like when she got really depressed and her husband kept telling her to go to a traditional healer we get to feel and see that she was upset and not in the right headspace with her hand covering her face and the background fading to black to show something is bothering her or she is dissociating.
